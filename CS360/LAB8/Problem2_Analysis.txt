Result Analysis:
The K-Means algorithm was applied to the 11 constituents of the WineQT dataset.
As the number of initial centroids (K) increases from 3 to 15, the clusters become smaller and more fragmented.
PCA was used strictly to project the 11-dimensional clusters down to 2 dimensions for visual inspection.
In the visualizations, lower values of K (like K=3 or K=5) show broad, distinct groupings that might correspond to general wine types or major quality categories.
Higher values (like K=10 or K=15) show heavy overlap in the 2D projected space, suggesting that they are finding very nuanced local structures or potentially over-segmenting the dataset.
