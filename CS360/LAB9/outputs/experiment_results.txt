CRITICAL COMPARATIVE OBSERVATIONS & ANALYSIS:
========================================================================================
1. Effect of Feature Scaling (1b vs 1c):
   - Without scaling, features like 'account_balance' (up to 100,000) and 'amount' (up to 10,000)
     completely overpower SGD updates. The algorithm saturates the sigmoid activation immediately,
     causing the model to predict the majority class (Fraud) for almost all samples.
     Non-fraud accuracy is practically zero (3.3%), with 26 False Positives out of 27 non-fraud cases.
   - With Z-score normalization, the loss contours are spherical. Gradient updates proceed uniformly,
     boosting non-fraud identification accuracy to 32.4% while maintaining 100% recall on fraud.

2. Model A vs Model B Comparison (1e-1g):
   - Model A (Amount + Frequency only) achieves 94.6% overall accuracy, but has 0% detection on non-fraud (TN=0, FP=27).
   - Model B (All Predictors) achieves 96.2% overall accuracy and correctly identifies true non-fraud transactions,
     demonstrating that account balance, total transaction volume, and time elapsed provide essential
     discriminative information that prevents false alarms.
   - Model B is objectively the superior predictive model for production fraud detection.
